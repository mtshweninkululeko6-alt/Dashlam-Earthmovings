About us

Your trusted partner in earthmoving support

Dashlam Earthmoving (Pty) Ltd is an earthmoving equipment maintenance, repair and equipment supply company committed to supporting the mining, construction, plant hire and industrial sectors.

Our services focus on helping clients maintain reliable equipment, minimise unplanned downtime and improve operational efficiency through practical maintenance solutions, technical support, component repairs and equipment supply.

We understand that equipment availability is critical to productivity and profitability. Our approach is centred on quality workmanship, responsive service, safety, reliability and building long-term relationships.

Vision

To become a trusted and recognised provider of earthmoving equipment maintenance, repair and supply solutions in South Africa's mining, construction and industrial sectors.

Mission

To provide dependable, cost-effective and quality-driven maintenance and equipment solutions that help clients operate safely and achieve production targets.

Safety Reliability Quality Integrity Customer service Continuous improvement
